Sync

Keeps one library across every device. The app pulls when it opens and after it regains focus, and saves back a few seconds after you stop editing. Your Spotify credentials and this device’s theme are deliberately left out of the file, so nothing secret ever reaches the repository.

Album lengths

Reads real running times from Spotify, so the target means something and each card can link straight to the album instead of a search. Create a free app, then paste its credentials here.

Bulk import

Add albums from a spreadsheet. Reads .xlsx and .csv. Either a sheet with Artist / Album / Genre / Runtime columns, or the original layout with one genre per column. Nothing is added until you confirm.
